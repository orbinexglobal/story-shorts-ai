Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

TinyPop TV operates a YouTube channel and publishes automatically generated short videos to it. This policy explains what little data is involved.

Information we access

  • The app connects to your own YouTube channel, with your explicit consent, to upload videos and (optionally) to count how many were published in a day. It does not read your private videos, comments, or personal information.
  • OAuth connection details (a refresh token) are stored as secret configuration and used only to keep the upload permission valid.

What we do with your data

  • Titles, descriptions, and generated stories are written into public video metadata as part of the uploaded Short.
  • No personal data is sold, shared, or used to build a profile of you.

Data retention and removal

  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all future uploads.
  • Videos you uploaded remain in your channel until you delete them there.
