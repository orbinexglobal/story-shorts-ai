  1,599 views, ~40-45% avg viewed each. Two more stories uploaded the same
  day got 1-2 views — consistent with same-hour batch-dump throttling (no

- Old BGMI gameplay Shorts (May-June 2024) get 1-3 views — the channel's
  only reach has come from the story Shorts, not the gameplay clips.
